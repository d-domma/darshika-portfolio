What it is

The project is the device and the program that runs it. This page is the software console: states have to happen in order, and every change is written to a log.

What you can do

  • Power on and watch a four-step self-test. A low battery stops in a fault state.
  • Mark pads connected and pass or fail a contact check.
  • Run analyze, download the log, and read the allowed transitions.

The analyze step ends the demo. No rhythm is read and no shock is sent.
