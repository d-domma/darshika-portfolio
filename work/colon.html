What it is

The project trains a convolutional network to look at images for signs associated with colon cancer. This build lets you load a picture and watch the first kind of computation: four filters, a ReLU, a pool, then a second layer on the combined maps.

What you can do

  • Upload an image and see it scaled and converted to grayscale.
  • Read a histogram, then the pooled feature maps.
  • Download mean, max, and energy for each map.
