What it is

The project trains convolutional networks on colorectal histology. A custom network with four convolutional layers, trained for 50 epochs, is compared with MobileNet, VGG16, ResNet50, and EfficientNetB1. Validation accuracy was 66.83%, then 71.71% after augmentation. EfficientNetB1 was the best reported test result, about 87%.

What you can do

  • Open one of the eight tissue classes and get a benign or malignant call.
  • Upload a patch and see the eight class scores.
  • Inspect the first feature maps and the five-model comparison.

The page runs the custom 4-layer CNN. It is not a diagnosis.
