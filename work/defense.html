What it is

The app is for the moment something feels wrong. It places the 911 call, texts a saved parent, and offers a route that can avoid higher-weight edges. The map uses public places around Cumming. Edge weights are samples for the demo, not the county registry and not anyone's address.

What you can do

  • Call 911 and text the first saved contact.
  • Start a check-in. If it expires, the text is ready. Nothing sends by itself.
  • Compare shortest and lower-weight routes, then save the trip.
