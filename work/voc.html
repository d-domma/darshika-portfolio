What it is

The device reads volatile organic compounds. The model work compared logistic regression, a vote-style forest, a time-series CNN, and XGBoost. This build keeps that comparison visible: seven raw markers, two ratios, total load, and temperature and humidity corrections, then three formulas side by side.

What you can do

  • Set temperature, humidity, and a seed, then run a sample.
  • Read every marker and the engineered features.
  • Open the vote list and the stump votes, and keep a short history.

The sensor is simulated here. The scores are not a clinical result.
