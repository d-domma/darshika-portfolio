What it is

LockAP is the project that already left the folder. This build is the study desk: a session timer, practice with an immediate answer, a short quiz that waits until the end to score, and notes kept per subject.

What you can do

  • Run a 25- or 50-minute session. Finished sessions are listed.
  • Practice across six subjects, or take a four-question quiz and review misses.
  • Watch a correct-rate table and write notes that stay in the browser.
