Finance Tracker

A personal account: more than one balance, a budget, a look at markets, and a chat that uses your own entries.

Open the build

RoleProduct

StackAngular originally, this build in the browser

This buildLedger, budgets, stocks, chat

What it is

It behaves like an account you keep yourself. Income, expenses, and transfers sit on named accounts. Budgets watch the month. A sample stock series gets a one-step line so the board can show a direction without pretending to be a brokerage.

What you can do

  • Add accounts, entries, transfers, and monthly repeats, then export CSV.
  • Set category limits and see what is left.
  • Read SMA and a fitted next step on sample prices.
  • Ask the chat about balance, spending, budgets, or a symbol.

Stock numbers are a sample series. The line is not advice.
