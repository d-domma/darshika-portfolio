Environmental Optimization AI

A design studio that takes a site and a set of preferences, checks them against open environmental data and building-code rules, and generates an editable building.

  • Wizard for lot, building type, roof, materials, and sustainability choices.
  • Climate, air quality, and nearby parks pulled from Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap.
  • Setbacks, height, coverage, floor-area ratio, open space, and stormwater checked before anything is modeled.
  • Blender writes a .blend, a web .glb, a preview, and an STL for 3D printing.

Women’s Defense

A safety app for getting help immediately, telling a parent, and choosing a route with the public record in view.

  • One action places a 911 call.
  • A text goes to a parent at the same time, so someone trusted knows what is happening.
  • Routing prefers safer streets instead of the shortest line on a map.
  • A map layer uses Forsyth County’s publicly published registry. This page does not reprint any name or address from that list.

Health

Colon Cancer Detection

Towards Precision Medicine in Colorectal Cancer: convolutional networks that separate eight tissue classes and call a patch benign or malignant.

  • A custom CNN with 4 convolutional layers, trained for 50 epochs.
  • Compared with MobileNet, VGG16, ResNet50, and EfficientNetB1.
  • Validation rose from 66.83% to 71.71% with augmentation. EfficientNetB1 reached about 87% on the test set.

VOC Detection

NeuroVOC, a science-fair build: a sensor workflow and a classifier for volatile organic compound signatures.

  • Seven raw biomarkers — hexanal, ethylbenzene, isoprene, acetone, octane, nonanal, and decane — plus ratio features and temperature and humidity corrections.
  • Model trials compared logistic regression, random forest, a CNN on the time series, and XGBoost.
  • The React app covers sign-in, device connection, a timed sample, results, and a local history.
