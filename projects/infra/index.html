What the pipeline does

  1. Reads lot size, building type, roof, materials, and sustainability options.
  2. Pulls climate, air quality, and nearby green space from open APIs.
  3. Checks setbacks, height, coverage, FAR, open space, and stormwater.
  4. Generates a Blender scene with landscape, PV, and a printable STL.
